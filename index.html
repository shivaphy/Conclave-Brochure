Evaluation Matrix

5-Parameter Peer Review Rubric (50 Points Total)

Every submission is evaluated against 5 independent criteria (10 points each; 1 = Poor, 5 = Average, 10 = Exceptional).

# Evaluation Parameter Evaluation Focus & Diagnostic Scope Weightage
40 – 50 Pts Band A
Accept As-Is

Ready for the publication compendium. Minor formatting verification only.

30 – 39 Pts Band B
Accept with Minor Revisions

Small adjustments to language, data presentation, or citations required.

20 – 29 Pts Band C
Conditional Major Revision

Substantial rewriting or deeper CO-PO data mapping required before re-review.

< 20 Pts Band D
Reject

Structurally flawed, out of scope, or lacking measurable attainment metrics.
